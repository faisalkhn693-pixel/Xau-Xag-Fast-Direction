XAU / XAG Trade Decision

LOCATION → SCENARIO → TRD → REACTION
CORE RULES
1. Location tells you where to watch — not automatically where to trade.
2. At VAH / VAL, look for rejection OR acceptance.
3. TRD = BIG BUBBLE + PRICE MOVED WITH IT.
4. Rejection + confirming TRD = reversal opportunity.
5. Acceptance beyond a major level + confirming TRD = continuation opportunity.
6. No clear TRD = WAIT.
7. Major gold news = NO TRADE.
8. One trade per setup.
9. Target 1:3 or better.
10. No early closing. BE only according to your plan.
