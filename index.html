XAU / XAG — FAST DIRECTION + LOCATION ENTRY CORE IDEA 6 Points = DIRECTION VWAP / VAH / VAL = LOCATION 5m Bubble/TRD = ENTRY CONFIRMATION Do not mix these three jobs. ⸻ 1. SIX DIRECTION POINTS Each point can only be: +1 = Bullish −1 = Bearish 0 = Neutral / unclear Maximum score: +6 / −6 ⸻ POINT 1 — DAILY VWAP * Price above Daily VWAP → +1 * Price below Daily VWAP → −1 VWAP is a direction point, NOT an automatic entry. ⸻ POINT 2 — 15m CVD Use the existing CVD swing-break rule. * Bullish CVD swing break → +1 * Bearish CVD swing break → −1 * No confirmed break → 0 Do not give a point for random CVD movement. ⸻ POINT 3 — D-POC Look at the Day chart and determine where the current D-POC has formed/moved. * D-POC formed/moved upward → +1 * D-POC formed/moved downward → −1 * D-POC is essentially where the day started → 0 ⸻ POINT 4 — 15m STRUCTURE Use confirmed 15m market-structure change. * Bullish CHoCH → +1 * Bearish CHoCH → −1 * No confirmed change → 0 Do not use tiny candle movements as structure. Once a direction is established, keep that point until a confirmed opposite CHoCH occurs. ⸻ POINT 5 — W-POC Compare the current Weekly POC with the previous Weekly POC. * Current W-POC above previous W-POC → +1 * Current W-POC below previous W-POC → −1 * Essentially unchanged → 0 This is the slower/higher-timeframe point. ⸻ POINT 6 — 5m TRD Use the existing TRD/Bubble system. * TRD+ → +1 * TRD− → −1 * No clear signal → 0 The Bubble itself does not automatically mean BUY or SELL. Price reaction must confirm it. ⸻ 2. DIRECTION / ELIGIBILITY +4 or higher BULLISH — ELIGIBLE −4 or lower BEARISH — ELIGIBLE Between −3 and +3 NOT ELIGIBLE Do not lower the ±4 threshold. The purpose of the new system is to make the six points faster and more useful, not simply to make eligibility easier. ⸻ 3. IMPORTANT — DIRECTION IS NOT ENTRY A +4/+5/+6 signal does NOT mean: BUY NOW A −4/−5/−6 signal does NOT mean: SELL NOW The six points only establish the directional permission. The actual entry requires a proper location and Bubble/TRD confirmation. ⸻ 4. VALID ENTRY LOCATIONS Only consider an entry when price reaches one of these important locations: Daily * Daily VWAP * Daily VAH * Daily VAL Weekly * Weekly VAH * Weekly VAL These are locations, not direction points. VAH/VAL should NOT be added to the six-point score. ⸻ 5. ENTRY RULE BUY All of the following should align: 1. Direction is BULLISH / +4 or better 2. Price reaches a valid location 3. 5m Bubble/TRD confirms bullish pressure 4. Price actually reacts/moves bullishly → BUY ⸻ SELL All of the following should align: 1. Direction is BEARISH / −4 or worse 2. Price reaches a valid location 3. 5m Bubble/TRD confirms bearish pressure 4. Price actually reacts/moves bearishly → SELL ⸻ 6. BUBBLE / TRD RULES Strong confirmation Large Bubble + price moves in the same direction Example: Large bullish Bubble → price actually pushes upward = useful confirmation. ⸻ DANGER Large Bubble + price does NOT move in that direction Example: Large bullish Bubble → price barely moves upward, gets absorbed, or starts moving down. = DANGER Do NOT blindly buy simply because the Bubble is large. The important thing is: Aggression + PRICE REACTION A large Bubble without price progress can indicate absorption/rejection. ⸻ 7. PERFECT LOCATION RULE When trading XAU and XAG: Trade the pair that is at the cleanest/perfect location. Leave the messy pair alone. Do not force both metals to give an entry. Example: XAU: * +5 * Perfect Daily VWAP location * Clean bullish Bubble/TRD confirmation XAG: * +5 * Between levels * Messy price action * Poor location → Trade XAU. Do not trade XAG just because its score is also +5. There is NO automatic XAG dominance. ⸻ 8. NO LOCATION = NO ENTRY Even if the score is: +6 If price is nowhere near a valid location: → WAIT Even if the score is: −6 If price is nowhere near a valid location: → WAIT The direction can be correct while the entry location is bad. ⸻ 9. NO BUBBLE/TRD CONFIRMATION = NO ENTRY Price reaching VWAP/VAH/VAL alone is NOT enough. Example: Price reaches Daily VWAP +5 bullish score but Bubble/TRD gives no bullish confirmation. → NO TRADE Wait. ⸻ 10. RANGE CONDITION Do NOT use TRE / TRN / RAN as another complicated scoring system. Instead, keep it simple: Directional market Price is making clear directional progress. → Normal location + Bubble/TRD entry can be used. Range Price is repeatedly rotating between areas without making meaningful directional progress. → Do NOT treat VWAP as an automatic trend-entry area. Wait for stronger confirmation from the location and 5m structure/order flow. The range condition changes the ENTRY METHOD, not the six-point direction. ⸻ 11. RANGE WARNING A market can move a large distance and then start rotating. Do not automatically call the whole movement a trend forever. What matters is the current behavior around the important levels. If price repeatedly moves back and forth through VWAP and fails to make directional progress: → Treat the current environment as range-like. ⸻ 12. MAJOR NEWS Major gold news: NO TRADE Especially when major scheduled news is close enough to make the normal technical reaction unreliable. ⸻ 13. TRADE MANAGEMENT One trade per setup. Do not keep entering repeatedly because the six-point score remains valid. Target 1:3 or better No early closing. Only: * TP * SL * BE when appropriate Do not close a winning trade early simply because price temporarily pulls back. ⸻ 14. THE COMPLETE DECISION PROCESS STEP 1 — CHECK DIRECTION Calculate the six points. Example: XAU +5 → Bullish eligible. ⸻ STEP 2 — WAIT FOR LOCATION Do NOT immediately enter. Wait for price to reach: * Daily VWAP * Daily VAH * Daily VAL * Weekly VAH * Weekly VAL ⸻ STEP 3 — WATCH BUBBLE/TRD At the location: Does Bubble/TRD confirm the directional bias? YES → continue. NO → wait. ⸻ STEP 4 — CHECK PRICE REACTION Does price actually respond in the Bubble/TRD direction? YES → entry can be considered. NO → danger / wait. ⸻ STEP 5 — CHOOSE THE CLEANER PAIR If both XAU and XAG qualify: Choose the pair with the best location + cleanest confirmation. Do not automatically choose XAG. ⸻ FINAL RULE DIRECTION → LOCATION → BUBBLE/TRD → PRICE REACTION → TRADE Not: SCORE → INSTANT ENTRY The six points tell you WHERE the market is likely leaning. The location tells you WHERE you want the trade. The Bubble/TRD tells you whether the immediate order flow supports the trade. Price reaction tells you whether that confirmation is actually working.
